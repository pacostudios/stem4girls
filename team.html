Our Leadership

The STEM4Girls team is composed of passionate educators, scientists, and advocates who are dedicated to inspiring young minds and promoting STEM education, especially for girls and underrepresented groups.

Our diverse team brings together expertise in education, scientific research, program management, and community outreach to create meaningful opportunities for students worldwide.

About STEM4Girls Organization

STEM4Girls is a non-profit corporation operating exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Our purpose is to encourage the pursuit of Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math – collectively termed STEM fields. We place particular emphasis on the encouragement and involvement of girls and other underrepresented groups in STEM fields.

🎯

Our Mission

To instill a love for STEM in girls across the globe and bridge the gap between students and the resources they need to grow their ideas.

🤝

Collaboration

Encouraging girls to establish active collaboration among peer groups, improving their outlook towards STEM and contribution to science.

📚

Education with Fun

Bringing knowledge with fun through educational workshops and camps designed to garner the interest of young minds.
