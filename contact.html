Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

When does registration open?

Registration opens on February 3, 2025, and proposals are due by May 5, 2025.

Is there a registration fee?

No! Participation in STEM4Sustainability is completely free for all students.

How many people can be on a team?

Teams must have 4-10 students with at least one female member.

Can international students participate?

Yes! This is a global competition open to students from all countries.

What are the prizes?

5 teams in each age category receive $250 research grants and present at IRCC 2025.

Where can I find project ideas?

Visit our Resources page for example projects across all STEM fields.
