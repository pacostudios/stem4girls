Why Your Support Matters

STEM4Girls is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to inspiring girls and underrepresented groups to pursue careers in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math. Every dollar you donate goes directly toward funding student research, providing grants, and organizing competitions that change lives.

$250 Maximum grant per outstanding project
3 School categories funded (Elementary, Middle, High)
100% Donations go directly to student programs
✅ 501(c)(3) Tax-Deductible Donation

Make a Difference Today

STEM4Girls is a non-profit corporation operating exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. All qualifying donations are tax-deductible.

Your contribution directly funds:

  • ♥ Student research grants of up to $250 per outstanding project
  • ♥ STEM competition organization and prizes across three school levels
  • ♥ Educational workshops and camps designed to inspire young minds
  • ♥ Scientific mentorship and expert review panels for student projects
  • ♥ Opportunities for students to present at the International Research Conference for Children (IRCC)
